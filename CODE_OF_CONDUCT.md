# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Zykit to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being kind, patient and respectful, especially to newcomers
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or in private
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling and personal or political attacks
- Publishing others’ private information without their explicit permission
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews) and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the maintainers through the contact options on the maintainer’s GitHub profile, or by opening a private security advisory if nothing else is available. Please don’t report conduct issues in public threads.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the reporter will be respected.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don’t follow this Code of Conduct. Depending on severity, responses range from a private warning, to a temporary ban, to a permanent ban from the project.
